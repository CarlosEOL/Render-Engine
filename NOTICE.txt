Render-Engine project notice

Render-Engine is an independent hobby project made for fun, learning, and
experimentation. The Gargantua scenario is an unofficial fan interpretation
inspired by Christopher Nolan's film Interstellar (2014). Render-Engine has no
affiliation with the film or its rights holders and claims no endorsement or
sponsorship. The film and associated intellectual property remain with their
respective rights holders.

# Galactic geometry and distant visibility — 12 November 2026

This follow-up addresses the parallel/mirrored Milky Way image reported during departure, missing distant galaxies, and the transition between top and side views. It supersedes the eight-layer exterior described in the earlier Milky Way reports.

## Findings and changes

## Scope and cost

The 256 new appearances are **background galaxies**, not 256 additional fully populated star catalogues. The existing four populated galaxies and two-million-star catalogue keep their IDs, seeds and locations. This change does not add new finder entries, planetary systems or gravity solvers for the background sample. The field is finite, not an infinite or observationally complete universe.

The Milky Way's expensive ray integration remains a bounded cached GPU pass. There is no new per-frame CPU stellar-position computation. Moving around an extended galaxy reveals a filtered volumetric approximation; the NASA/JPL source remains an artist concept. No measured frame-rate or hardware-temperature improvement is claimed.

## Validation

Both final **Release x64** suites passed: DX12 produced **228 scene and 13 UI captures**; OpenGL produced **219 scene and 13 UI captures**. Each reported **zero failed assertion locations**. DX12 additionally reported **zero graphics validation errors**. These are correctness checks, not performance benchmarks.

- All three isolated outward views had zero lit pixels on both renderers, including the shallow radial-edge view that must not show a backwards image.
- The 19-view inclination sweep passed. Adjacent 5° integrated-display ratios varied continuously; DX12 ranged from approximately 0.683 to 0.998. Foreshortening and extinction still change total displayed light near edge-on; the renderer does not force constant brightness.
- The isolated DX12 galaxy field had 4,680 lit pixels at magnitude 12 and 4,444 at the default magnitude 7. Magnitude 4 changed the image, and repeated static renders were identical.
- Each catalogue host retained a visible central image face-on and edge-on. In DX12 the two disk hosts retained 1,345 and 1,293 lit samples within the central 40×40-pixel patch when edge-on; the elliptical host retained all 1,600. Face-on all three retained 1,600.
- Existing cache continuity, continued-flight scheduling and settled idle reuse passed. Atmosphere, exposure/flare exclusion, independent Kerr capture, spacecraft/interior, wormhole and resize checks passed. Native DX12 exercised 40 actual DLSS evaluations with nine history resets.

The README includes actual isolated oblique/edge-on renders and a distant-galaxy field capture. Capture isolation deliberately omits unrelated stars, planets and selection markers so the tested galaxy geometry is visible. Existing size-conversion warnings remain.

The retained launchers are `build/dx12/Release/bin/Render-Engine-DX12.exe` and `build/current/bin/Render-Engine.exe`. Final logs/PNG evidence are retained under the standard `build/latest-*-render.log` and `build/latest-*-checks/` paths after removing superseded captures and validated raw-PPM duplicates. Source, dependencies, current object caches and historical text logs remain. No branch was switched or published.

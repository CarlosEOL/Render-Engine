# Miller atmosphere and lunar material fixes — November 12, 2026

| Files | Change | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `src/BlackHole.cpp` | Classify the complete atmospheric shell using the same foreground distance as the lens depth guard. Composite foreground shells after Kerr lensing, including orbital cameras; leave distant/background shells in the lensed scene. | The previous fix only moved air after lensing inside 1.03 planet radii. From orbit and just above that cutoff, the rim still entered the screen warp and smeared into unrelated sky pixels. Nearby air is foreground scattering, even when the camera is outside it. |
| `src/BlackHole.h` | Add an atmosphere enable switch and read-only lens-source depth access for rendering diagnostics. Defaults preserve normal rendering. | Paired renders isolate atmospheric contributions; real depth identifies ship geometry without mistaking grey disk emission for a spacecraft. There is no new per-frame readback in normal play. |
| `src/StarSystem.h`, `src/SolarSystem.cpp` | Give planet materials an explicit palette, defaulting to the existing climate palette; assign Earth's Moon a neutral lunar palette. | Terrain relief and surface classification should not force Earth-like vegetation/desert/snow colours onto the Moon. Other worlds retain their existing materials. |
| `src/StarSystem.cpp` | Keep lunar displacement, collision heights and 100 m ground detail unchanged; shade seeded maria/highland albedo variations in neutral regolith colours independently of elevation. | Preserve the desired hills while removing climate colour substitutions. This is procedural lunar material, not a mapped lunar survey or crater catalogue. |
| `src/RenderChecks.cpp` | Add a sunlit lunar colour check and paired atmospheric-on/off views at 3, 1.029 and 1.031 planet radii. Check linear HDR differences against analytic shell intersections, finite output and nonempty atmospheric contribution. Use depth-selected linear HDR pixels for the spacecraft spin-invariance check; report the first earlier failed check. | Verify that local air cannot affect unrelated rays, cover the old hard cutoff, and avoid false failures from grey background emission in the previous colour-only ship mask. Readback remains opt-in test work. |
| `README.md`, this record | Link and explain the fixes and their verification. | Keep each change and its reason reviewable. |

The original layering was deliberately rebuilt and tested against the new checks: it altered **5,104 unrelated sky pixels** in the orbital view and **6,736** just above the old altitude cutoff. Restoring the fix reduces both to **zero**. The three corrected atmospheric views contain no nonfinite HDR colour components. The lunar sample has 64,814 neutral pixels out of 64,890 illuminated pixels (99.88%).

Debug x64 builds successfully. The full OpenGL suite passes **88 captures with zero GL errors**, including **2,593 depth-identified ship pixels** unchanged across spin settings. These fixes apply to the complete application; the native DX12 preview does not yet have planetary atmospheres or lunar surfaces. They must be preserved by the full-scene DX12 port rather than reintroducing the old composition/material rules.

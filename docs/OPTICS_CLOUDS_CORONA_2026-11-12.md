## Magnitude is a brightness limit

The estimate is `m_bol = 4.74 - 2.5 log10(L/Lsun) + 5 log10(d_pc/10)`. Larger limits admit fainter sources; this is not a fixed radius. A luminous distant star can be visible when a closer faint star is excluded. The [NASA magnitude activity](https://imagine.gsfc.nasa.gov/science/activities/try_l2/supernovae.html) explains the logarithmic brightness scale; the [SDSS explanation](https://voyages.sdss.org/preflight/light/magnitudes/) describes apparent and absolute magnitude.

The synthetic catalogue supplies total luminosity, so this is a **bolometric estimate**, not a calibrated V-band limit. It does not yet include each sightline's dust extinction or spectral bolometric correction. The display PSF has an artistic visibility floor after selection; the magnitude criterion itself has no unconditional far-star visibility floor. The sparse representative catalogue cannot reproduce all real naked-eye stars. Lower limits reduce point rasterization/fragment work; they do not claim to eliminate vertex processing or provide a measured reduction in GPU temperature.

## Accuracy boundaries

Coronal streamers and prominence shapes are visual plasma approximations, not magnetohydrodynamic solutions. Earth clouds use original deterministic noise, not satellite weather or measured meteorology. Atmosphere multiple scattering/self-shadowing remain bounded approximations. The real Earth reflects sunlight; excluding its reflected highlights from this flare model is a classification choice, not a statement that Earth absorbs all incoming light.

HDR here denotes scene highlight compression for an ordinary display, not a promise of Windows HDR10 output. Pure exposure and HDR intentionally have different rendering goals. SpaceEngine screenshots guide the requested appearance; they do not reveal its private exposure algorithm, source code, or actual calibration.

The two original generated assets are recorded in `assets/stellar/corona.prompt.md` and `assets/ui/laboratories.prompt.md`. Neither is copied from the reference screenshots. Menu art is illustrative and is not a scale diagram or evidence of the simulation's rendered output.
